# About This Project

The following is a dump of text related to this project. It needs to be sorted into pages. Note, I call this concept "Local Commonships" in some of my notes which is a local verson of Commonships of the Future, a play on Mihaly Csikszentmihalyi's "Fellowship of the Future".

---

Within an Evolutionary Design Group, we use a collaborative creative practice as the basis for how we operate. Like a personal creative practice, a collaborative one does these steps, but rather than having one person making all the decisions, we decide together.

- Find work that needs doing
- Choose what’s OURS to do
- Do the work
- Communicate what we learn to the world

## An invitation to join an evolutionary design group

I am looking for people interested in forming a Cultural Creators' Group in Broomfield.

A Cultural Creators' Group is a diverse group of individuals who dedicate a portion of their creative practice to collaboratively help create a better world. Please visit the [Cultural Creators' Group](https://culturalcreatorsgroup.org) website for more information.

We all have a creative practice, but for most of us it's disorganized and undirected. This group will explore how we can transform our creative energy into cultural artifacts that are both meaningful to ourselves and useful to others. As cultural creators, we'll strive to use our creative practice to improve the world around us, stretching and improving ourselves in the process; we'll then have greater capacities for the next project. In this way, we'll improve ourselves while improving the world in a virtuous cycle. 

This group is intentionally local, so we will meet in person on a weekly basis and concentrate on improving Broomfield and its surrounding areas. To help similar groups in other locations, we will also share what we learn with the rest of the world.

This is a group that embraces a comprehensive world view. As such, any political action we might take would be minimal and nonpartisan. Instead, we'll be focused on creating solutions to problems by designing and building something useful.

## EDG as part of my creative practice

It seems like the best way I could move forward is to start living the full set of ideas that are in my book, and that would mean not just working on my own projects, but gathering a group of people to work locally on projects that will improve the community.

I think this is an extension of the idea of putting together a men's group in that it will connect me to people that care about the same things I do, it will give us a platform to improve ourselves as well as improve the community, and it wouldn't be limited to men.

It would also be a place where my ideas could be tested and refined, and that could be very valuable. And it could be a generator of open artifacts projects.

First, I'm not sure about the fragility of values. In his talk on "Indigenous Thinking for Troubled Times," Tyson Yunkaport talked about indigenous knowledge as being based in what he called "natural law," although he warned about labelling it. The idea is that these are human laws that are inherent in ourselves and our relationship with Earth and are more stable than the kinds of values we try to enforce through law. They are also different than the kinds of behaviors you see in people who are operating in systems that are divorced from nature, like our western civilization. He refers to those people as "domesticated humans." I think the ideas in this book could be seen as trying to help people get past their domestication and embrace their natural humanness—to set them free, as MJ Fitzpatrick would say.

Second, I feel like the Rebel Wisdom community is a pretty good Fellowship of the Future contender. Most likely, such a fellowship will be collection or a network of communities, so it could be looked as a Network of Sensemakers (), though they would serve a different function. I think Rebel Wisdom started as a node in a potential Network of Sensemakers (), but how now added features which make it part of a potential Fellowship of the Future ().

## Local Commonships pattern notes

*Act locally as part of groups applying global ideas to local problems and conditions.*

When your focus is global, it’s not always easy to see how you can best act locally or find the resources you need; you need a local group to act locally.

Therefore:
Assemble and participate in local groups of world creators charged with applying the principles and lessons shared within global commonships to local problems and conditions.

improving the world through [[Atlas/Patterns/Design]] can be done at several scales; generalized designs can be developed as [[Atlas/Notes/Open Artifacts]] projects, but ultimately the world won’t change until those designs are built locally.

In a world where you can create communities of people spread around the world, this is about local connection and local action. There is a real need for face to face interactions, and for people who are committed to the success of their local community.

Local commonships would be groups of people dedicated to working toward creating a better world through design and embracing complexity. In essence, they are [[Commonships of the Future]] focused on the future of a local region, city, or neighborhood.

I don’t necessarily think of this group as being as small as a functional group like [[Atlas/Notes/Evolutionary Design Teams]], but small functional groups could come together temporarily to tackle specific local problems. If, for example, you were to say that the Rebel Wisdom community was what Csikszentmihalyi referred to as the “Fellowship of the Future,” then local commonships would be like local Rebel Wisdom communities, grouped by physical location so they could act within that local region, city or neighborhood.

I think that the small functional groups coming out of the Local Commonships would be tied to [[Atlas/Notes/Open Artifacts]] projects, pulling down information and contributing improvements based on their experience.
